# CreditKasa Internal FAQ for Support Agents

## Loan Application Questions

### Q: What is the maximum loan amount?
A: The maximum loan amount for new clients is 10,000 UAH. For returning clients with a positive repayment history (at least 2 successfully repaid loans), the limit is 25,000 UAH.

### Q: What are the interest rates?
A: The standard interest rate is 1.9% per day for new clients and 0.01% per day for returning clients on their first repeat loan (promotional rate, valid for up to 5,000 UAH). All interest rates are clearly shown to the client before they confirm the loan.

### Q: How long does it take to receive the funds?
A: For approved applications, funds are typically disbursed within 15 minutes to the client's bank card. In rare cases (new card verification, large amounts), it can take up to 2 hours.

### Q: Can a client have multiple active loans?
A: No. A client may only have one active loan at any time. A new loan can only be issued after the previous one is fully repaid.

## Repayment Questions

### Q: How can a client repay the loan?
A: Clients can repay via:
1. Bank card (Visa/Mastercard) through the personal cabinet on the website.
2. Terminal payment (PrivatBank, iBox, EasyPay).
3. Bank transfer to our company account (details provided in the loan agreement).

### Q: What happens if a client overpays?
A: Overpayments are automatically credited to the client's balance in our system. The client can request a refund to their bank card within 30 calendar days by contacting support.

### Q: Can a client extend the loan term?
A: Yes. A client can extend the loan term by 7, 14, or 30 days by paying the accrued interest for the current period plus an extension fee of 1% of the remaining principal. Extensions must be requested before the loan due date.

## Account & Security Questions

### Q: How does a client reset their password?
A: The client can use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. A one-time code will be sent to their registered phone number via SMS.

### Q: What should I do if a client reports unauthorized access?
A: Immediately freeze the client's account, log the incident in the CRM under "Security Incident", and escalate to the IT Security team within 1 hour. Do NOT process any loan applications or disbursements for this account until the security team clears it.
