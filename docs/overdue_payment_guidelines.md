# CreditKasa Internal Guidelines: Overdue Payment Handling

## 1. Classification of Overdue Status

All overdue loans are classified into three tiers:

- **Soft Overdue (1-14 days):** Automatic SMS and email reminders are sent. No penalties are accrued during the first 3 days (grace window). Starting from day 4, a daily penalty of 0.5% of the overdue amount is charged.
- **Medium Overdue (15-60 days):** The account is flagged for active collections. The support agent must attempt at least 3 phone contacts. The daily penalty increases to 1% of the overdue amount.
- **Hard Overdue (61-90 days):** The case is escalated to the Legal & Collections department. All communication with the client must be logged in the CRM. No further lending is allowed to this client until the debt is fully settled.

## 2. Communication Scripts for Support Agents

### First Contact (Soft Overdue)
"Доброго дня, [Ім'я клієнта]. Це [Ім'я агента] з CreditKasa. Ми помітили, що платіж за кредитом [Номер кредиту] на суму [Сума] грн прострочений. Чи можемо ми допомогти вам вирішити цю ситуацію?"

### Escalation Contact (Medium Overdue)
"Доброго дня, [Ім'я клієнта]. Ваш кредит [Номер кредиту] має прострочення більше 15 днів. Нагадуємо, що щоденна пеня складає 1% від суми заборгованості. Ми рекомендуємо розглянути варіант реструктуризації. Бажаєте дізнатися більше?"

## 3. Partial Payment Policy
- If the client can pay at least 30% of the overdue amount immediately, the agent is authorized to freeze penalties for 7 calendar days.
- If the client can pay 50% or more, the agent may offer a penalty waiver (see Loan Restructuring Policy, Section 2).
- All partial payments must be recorded in the CRM with a note referencing the agreed timeline.

## 4. Escalation Rules
- If the client is unreachable for 5 consecutive business days during Medium Overdue, the case must be auto-escalated to Hard Overdue tier regardless of the actual number of overdue days.
- If the client explicitly states they will not pay, the agent must immediately log the conversation transcript and escalate to the Legal team.
