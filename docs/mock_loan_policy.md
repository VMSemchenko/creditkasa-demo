# CreditKasa Internal Guidelines: Loan Restructuring

## 1. Eligibility for Restructuring
Clients are eligible for a loan restructuring program if they meet all the following conditions:
- The delay in payment (overdue) is between 15 and 90 days.
- The client has documented proof of a sudden loss of income (e.g., termination of employment, severe medical condition).
- The client has not applied for restructuring in the past 12 months.

## 2. Restructuring Options
If eligible, the following options can be offered to the client:
- **Term Extension:** Extending the loan term by up to 6 months, recalculating the monthly payment to a lower amount.
- **Grace Period:** Providing a 1-month "holiday" where the client only pays the interest, and the principal is deferred to the next month.
- **Penalty Waiver:** If the client repays at least 50% of the overdue principal immediately, all accrued late penalties will be waived.

## 3. Application Process
1. The client must submit a formal request via email to `support@creditkasa.com` with the subject "Restructuring Request - [Loan ID]".
2. The client must attach relevant documents (e.g., dismissal letter, hospital bill).
3. The support agent must verify the documents within 48 hours and submit the request to the Risk Department.

## 4. Rejection Criteria
A request must be immediately rejected and escalated to the Collections team if:
- The overdue period exceeds 90 days.
- The client provides falsified documents.
- The client refuses to communicate via official phone channels when requested by the agent.
